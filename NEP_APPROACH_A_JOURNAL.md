- Code/trace correlation clarifies that the extra existing-Megatron NCCL time is primarily scheduler serialization and participant-arrival skew, not demonstrated raw bandwidth interference. Each layer Gather, EDP reduction, and Scatter are enqueued serially on one shared NEP dispatch stream; `_wait_for_nep_dispatch_launch()` then makes the compute/current stream wait on the layer completion event before the next model-EP boundary. The overlapping participant sets differ (`nep_owner_transfer` pair `[0,4]`, `ep_dp` pair `[0,8]`, regular EP group `[0-7]`), so pair/owner barriers align local phases but can deliver the four pairs to the next full EP collective at different times. An early EP NCCL kernel then records long residency while waiting for the other EP ranks.
- Dense-DP overlap is explicitly disabled for the NCCL NEP path: parameter hooks record dense readiness without calling the normal Megatron `start_grad_sync`, and `_start_delayed_dense_grad_syncs()` launches all dense buckets only from `finish_grad_sync()` after the NEP drain. Its extra tail is therefore an implementation constraint, not evidence that reshard traffic made the same dense all-reduce intrinsically slower. `NCCL_LAUNCH_ORDER_IMPLICIT=1` may add cross-communicator ordering pressure, but the current evidence does not isolate it as the dominant performance cause; CUDA connection count is not causal on Blackwell.
